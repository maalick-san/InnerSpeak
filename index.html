Questions

Before you begin

Do I need any experience with energy work?

None at all. Most clients arrive with nothing but curiosity. Every session is guided from beginning to end — all you need to do is arrive and breathe.

What should I wear or bring?

Comfortable, loose clothing. Everything else — including a quiet, beautiful space — is taken care of.

Can sessions be done remotely?

Yes. Energy is not limited by distance; remote sessions are held over a video call and follow the same structure as in-person work.

How many sessions will I need?

Many feel a real shift after a single cleansing. Deeper, older blockages usually soften over three to four sessions — which is why the Full Alignment Journey exists.

Is this a replacement for medical care?

No. Energy work is a complement to — never a substitute for — professional medical or psychological care.

Stress, grief and old patterns can settle in the body as heaviness or restlessness. Chakra work is a gentle way to release them.

Seven centres, one flow.

Select a centre to see what it governs, and how a blockage there tends to feel.

Crown, at the top of the head

Sahasrara

Connection, meaning and a sense of something larger than yourself.

In flow

Life feels purposeful. You trust where you are going, even without a map.

When blocked

A feeling of being adrift or cut off, and days that seem to lack meaning.

Third eye, between the brows

Ajna

Intuition, clarity and the ability to see a situation as it is.

In flow

Decisions come easily. You notice your instincts and feel able to follow them.

When blocked

Overthinking, mental fog, and second-guessing what you already know.

Throat, at the base of the neck

Vishuddha

Expression, honesty and being heard.

In flow

You say what you mean, calmly, and you listen as well as you speak.

When blocked

Swallowed words, a fear of speaking up, or a tight feeling in the throat.

Heart, at the centre of the chest

Anahata

Love, compassion and the ability to give and receive.

In flow

You feel open and warm toward others, and kind toward yourself.

When blocked

Guardedness, grief that lingers, and difficulty letting people in.

Solar plexus, in the upper abdomen

Manipura

Confidence, willpower and a sense of your own worth.

In flow

You act with quiet confidence and keep the promises you make to yourself.

When blocked

Self-doubt, indecision, or a need to control everything around you.

Sacral, in the lower abdomen

Svadhisthana

Creativity, pleasure and emotional flow.

In flow

Ideas arrive freely, and you allow yourself to enjoy things without guilt.

When blocked

Emotional numbness, guilt, or a long creative dry spell.

Root, at the base of the spine

Muladhara

Safety, grounding and the feeling of belonging.

In flow

You feel steady and at home in your body, whatever the day brings.

When blocked

Restlessness, worry about security, and a sense of being unrooted.

What a session feels like.

  1. Arrive and settle

    We begin with conversation and breath. You share what feels heavy, and we set an intention together.

  2. Reading your energy

    A quiet scan of the seven centres shows where energy moves freely and where it has come to rest.

  3. Cleansing and release

    Guided breath, sound and energy work help blockages soften. Many people describe a feeling of warmth or lightness.

  4. Integration

    You leave with simple rituals to keep your balance, and support whenever you need it.

The practice behind Innerspeak.

Innerspeak began with a simple belief: your energy is always speaking. Most of us have only stopped listening.

Each session is led personally and one-to-one. There is no script and no rush. The work is to help you hear what is already there, and to clear whatever stands in its way.

Before you begin.

Do I need any experience with energy work?

None at all. Every session is guided from beginning to end. All you need to do is arrive and breathe.

What should I wear or bring?

Comfortable, loose clothing. Everything else is taken care of.

Can sessions be done remotely?

Yes. Remote sessions are held over a video call and follow the same structure as in-person work.

How many sessions will I need?

It varies from person to person. Some come for a single cleansing, while older patterns often take several sessions, which is why the Full Alignment Journey exists.

Is this a replacement for medical care?

No. Energy work complements professional medical or psychological care. It does not replace it.

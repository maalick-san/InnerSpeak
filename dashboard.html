04 Key Takeaways

Reading the 5-Month Pattern

Website Trajectory

Traffic declined Jan→Mar (13K→11K active users), then surged in April to a 5-month peak of 16K driven by Q1 results, bonus share announcements, and the Shahba Bank deal. May settled slightly to 15K while engagement rate hit its 5-month high of 56.2% — more qualified traffic, not just volume.

Instagram Followers

Followers grew every single month without exception: 11,470 → 12,180 (+6.2% over 5 months). This baseline growth is independent of content volume — even low-post months like March and May still gained ~80–160 new followers.

LinkedIn B2B Power

105.3K company followers by May — 8.6x the size of Instagram audience. YTD growth of +10,863 followers (+11.3%) with all 5 months positive. April spike (+3,109) signals successful awareness campaign; May sustained at +2,815. LinkedIn is the dominant B2B channel for recruitment, investor relations, and stakeholder engagement.

Views Are Event-Driven

Monthly views swung wildly: 60.9K → 187.9K (Feb, Estithmar Capital launch) → 33.3K → 88.7K → 78.9K. The pattern shows two distinct spikes tied to major corporate announcements; baseline (non-announcement months) sits around 33–60K.

Stories Are the Backbone

Stories have been the most consistent content type, running 28–56/month across the period. Posts and reels are sporadic (0–7/month). In May, 53 stories were published vs. just 1 post — Stories carry the daily presence.

Content Volume ≠ Reach

February had 60 pieces of content and 187.9K views. March had 37 pieces and only 33.3K views. Content quality and announcement timing matter far more than posting frequency. The single Elegancia FM post in May drove 11.2K impressions alone.

Organic Search is Rising

Google organic overtook direct traffic in May (7.9K vs 6.4K sessions). Across the 5 months, Doha, Qatar remains the primary audience city, with consistent secondary markets in Riyadh, Ashburn, and Dubai — useful for campaign targeting.

03 Key Takeaways

Al Maha Island · 5-Month Pattern

Website — Seasonal Dip then Recovery

Views dropped sharply Jan→Mar (17.5K→4.8K) as winter season ended. From April, consistent month-on-month recovery: +18.9% Apr, +26.2% May. Qatar remains the dominant audience at 61–69% of all visitors; top destination page is /carbone at 7–9% of views.

Instagram — May Breakout

After 4 months of declining views and reach, May reversed sharply: +110% IG views, +282% avg reach/day. Driven by an 8-post GCC geo-targeting campaign (Dammam, Muscat, Khobar, Riyadh, Abu Dhabi) with the Dammam post alone hitting 52.3K views and 27.4K reach.

Follower Base Stable but Eroding

59.1K→59.2K across 5 months — virtually flat. Jan–Mar showed small gains (+224, +174, +144). Apr–May flipped negative (−122, −152). Net YTD: +40 followers. Off-season unfollows are offsetting new gains; follower growth strategy needed for summer.

GCC Geo-Campaign is the Playbook

May's content strategy — city-specific "welcome" posts for GCC audiences (Dammam, Muscat, Khobar, Riyadh, Abu Dhabi) — generated the highest reach in 5 months. Avg reach per post hit 5,440 vs a prior baseline near zero. This format should be systematically replicated.

Stories Are Volume, Posts Are Reach

January ran 289 stories but 0 posts, producing high volume but limited reach per piece (avg ~197). May ran just 90 stories but 8 posts and 5 reels — generating 5.9× higher avg reach per post. Shift from story-only to post+reel strategy is paying off.

Content Gap: Mar & Apr

March had only 1 post, April had 0. These months coincided with the lowest reach and views of the period. The correlation is direct: zero posts = invisible on the feed. A minimum 4-post cadence even in off-season would protect baseline reach.
